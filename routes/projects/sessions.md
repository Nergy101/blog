# 🗓️ Sessions — Collaborative session planning

Sessions is a collaborative dashboard for collecting session ideas, finding speakers, and keeping track of upcoming and previously presented sessions.

## Live Site

Visit [sessions.nergy.space](https://sessions.nergy.space).

## What it does

- Collects proposed topics and ideas in one place
- Lets speakers claim ideas they’d like to present
- Shows upcoming sessions and planned talks
- Keeps an archive of sessions that have already been presented
