# 🐸 Emo Kikkers — Frog-themed party games and creative tools

Emo Kikkers is a playful collection of frog-themed games and creative tools. Make a custom frog, play party games together, and download the visual assets.

## Live Site

Visit [emokikkers.nl](https://emokikkers.nl).

## What’s there

- **Party games** — play games like Slootrace, Kwaakwerk, Quiz, and Spreekbeurt
- **Kikker Maker** — create a custom frog to use in the games
- **Visual assets** — browse and download the frog illustrations and branding assets
- **Year in review** — revisit shared moments, photos, and awards in the Wrapped overview
